Three things to know first

Muscle answers effort, at any age

  1. Strength fades faster than muscle size, and it matters more

    With age, strength falls two to five times faster than muscle mass. In older adults, losing strength is more closely linked with disability and earlier death than losing muscle size.

  2. Your muscles still respond, at 50, 70 and beyond

    Across 121 in older adults, progressive made people substantially stronger and better at everyday tasks such as getting out of a chair. Women's muscles respond to training much as men's do.

  3. Training and protein work together

    Strength training is the main signal for muscle to grow. Enough protein, spread across your meals, helps you make the most of it.

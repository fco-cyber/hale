Exercise library

Fourteen movements that cover the whole body: legs, hips, pushing, pulling, carrying, trunk and balance. Open any one for a moving figure, the muscles it works, step-by-step instructions, and easier and harder versions.

Each figure shows the start position faintly and the finish position in colour, with the working limbs highlighted. Inside each exercise the figure moves slowly through the repetition; it stops when it's off screen, if you choose Still in Colours and display, or if your device asks for reduced motion.
